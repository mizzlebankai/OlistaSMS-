Olistar School privacy notice

This operational notice explains how the School Portal handles school administration data. It should be reviewed and approved by the school’s privacy lead or legal adviser before publication.

Information we use

Admissions, student, guardian, attendance, fee, timetable, and academic records may be collected to administer education, communicate with families, and produce school reports.

Access and sharing

Access is limited by account role. Information is stored with the school’s configured Firebase and Netlify service providers and should not be shared outside authorized school purposes unless required by law or authorized by the school.

Retention and deletion

The school should define retention periods for admissions, academic, financial, and safeguarding records. Authorized administrators can delete portal records; deletion requests may be subject to legal, accounting, safeguarding, or archival obligations.

Your questions

For access, correction, or deletion questions, contact the school administrator through the school’s official communication channel.
